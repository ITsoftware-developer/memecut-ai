# Memebibliotheek

De tien PNG-stickers zijn speciaal voor MemeCut AI gemaakt met de ingebouwde ImageGen-tool. Ze zijn afzonderlijk uitgesneden uit één rasterbeeld. De bestanden hebben een transparante achtergrond en meten 384 × 384 pixels. De editor gebruikt de namen, tags en beschrijvingen uit `catalog.json` om een passende reactie te vinden.

## Gebruikte prompt

```text

True transparent alpha background. Equal size cells. All art including white die-cut outlines and little accents fits inside the central 68 percent of each cell, with wide completely transparent gaps between cells. Each sticker is a very expressive original cartoon gamer creature, roughly head and hands at most, indie pixel-art and hand-drawn arcade arcade sticker hybrid, bold deep plum outlines, colorful detailed shading, clean white 5px sticker keyline, at thumbnail size highly legible, zero lettering, typography, watermarks, brand, recognizable franchise character or grids. Exactly these subjects in reading order:
TOP ROW LEFT TO RIGHT: (1) a copper fox flopped flat on its face with a tiny dizzy star, comedic faceplant and fail, orange; (2) a round lavender ghost with very wide eyes and open screaming mouth, sudden jumpscare, lilac; (3) a pink axolotl burying its face in one hand, obvious cringe facepalm, rose; (4) a gold cat raising a small trophy above its head, genuine victory, gold; (5) a teal chameleon leaning toward the camera with one giant incredulous eye and raised eyebrow, confused double take, teal.
BOTTOM ROW LEFT TO RIGHT: (6) a lime frog with head thrown back laughing and two teardrops, contagious laughter, green; (7) a small pale skull staring blankly with its mouth a flat straight line, awkward silence, ivory; (8) a red panda peering sharply sideways from under a lowered brow, suspicious side-eye, rust and burgundy; (9) a blue penguin with a large nervous sweat drop and teeth clenched, high-stakes tension, cool blue; (10) a grinning cream rabbit clutching a four-leaf clover with tiny green sparkles, got lucky, mint and chartreuse. Keep every sticker distinct, isolated, centered and consistently scaled, each about the same visual weight. Plain transparency in all unused regions, no background shadow or checkerboard.
```
